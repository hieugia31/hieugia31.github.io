## 🛠️ How It Works

MacMiniMode uses macOS power management features to prevent sleep while the lid is closed. It watches whether the MacBook is connected to power. When power is connected and you have the mode enabled, it applies the keep-awake behavior. When power is removed, it releases that behavior so the MacBook can sleep normally again.

You do not need to configure anything technical. Open the app, use the menu bar item, and turn the mode on. Keep the MacBook plugged in for the mode to remain active. Close the lid and the MacBook keeps running.

## 🔋 Battery Behavior Explained

Keeping a closed-lid laptop awake uses power. MacMiniMode avoids the most common mishap by tying the behavior to the power adapter. While plugged in, the MacBook has a steady power source. While on battery, the behavior stops so the machine can follow its normal sleep rules.

For best results, keep the MacBook connected to power during long unattended tasks. If the machine is on battery, expect normal laptop sleep behavior rather than always-on operation.
